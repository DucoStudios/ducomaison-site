The problem

Measuring by hand wastes material — and your time.

You've got a stack of plywood, a pile of lumber, or an oddly-shaped slab, and a list of pieces to cut from it. Doing that math in your head — or worse, on a scrap of paper — means wasted sheets, mismatched pieces, and a second trip to the lumber yard.

The fix

See the whole layout before you cut anything.

CutBoard puts your boards on a real-scale canvas. Drag pieces into place, let auto-arrange pack them tight, and print a labelled cut sheet — so every cut is planned before the saw ever turns on.

Why print it

Leave your phone in the house. Bring the paper.

Sawdust and touchscreens don't mix. Plan the whole layout on your laptop or tablet from wherever's comfortable, then print the cut list once — you don't need to bring any screen anywhere near the saw. No dust in the charging port, no sawdust ground into the glass, no risk of dropping it in a pile of offcuts. Your device stays clean and working like new for years longer, and the paper doesn't care about a little dust.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Does it really work for any type of board?

Yes. Rectangular sheets and lumber work out of the box with exact dimensions. For anything irregular — a live-edge slab, reclaimed timber, an odd offcut — import a photo, calibrate the real size against a known measurement, then trace the outline and any crack or void zones.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. Open the tool and start — your project autosaves in your browser. You only need an email if you subscribe to Pro (for cloud save and billing).

Can I use it on my phone or tablet?

It works on a phone, but the side panels take up a lot of a small screen, so we'd recommend a tablet or desktop for actually building a layout. Your phone is fine for a quick check or pulling up the parts list mid-project.

What happens to my projects if I cancel Pro?

Your local, in-browser projects are untouched — those were never tied to a subscription. Cloud-saved projects stay in your account but become read-only until you resubscribe.

What's your refund / cancellation policy?

Cancel anytime from the account panel in the app — no phone calls, no retention flow. Subscribing gives you immediate access to Pro features, which under EU consumer law means you're agreeing to waive the standard 14-day withdrawal period for this digital subscription (you'll see this noted at checkout).

Is my data private?

Your project data stays in your browser unless you're on Pro and choose to save it to the cloud. Slab photos never leave your device unless you save that project to the cloud too.
