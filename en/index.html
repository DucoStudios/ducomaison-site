Your own portal, around the clock

You never need to wonder how the house is doing. After every visit, everything is gathered in your personal portal: photo reports, electricity and water meter readings (and pool pH if you have a pool) with automatic comparison to the previous visit, so you see immediately if anything has changed. No meter readings to interpret yourself, we read them and keep track for you. See the full visit history, what's planned ahead, and the status of any renovation projects, all in one place. Need an extra visit beyond your plan? Book and pay for it directly in the portal. No login details to remember, a simple link in the email is enough. Included in all three packages.

Basic Pool Care, from €20/month

Have a pool? We skim off leaves and debris, empty skimmer and pump baskets, check the water level, inspect the pump and filter, and measure pH which is logged in your report. Chemical balancing and repairs are instead handled via project management, with a local pool company. Price follows your watching level: €20/25/30 per month at 4/6/8 visits a year.

Project management, a standalone service

Need a tradesperson, a quote, or help keeping track of a larger project? We handle the contact and coordination for a fixed fee on the supplier's invoice, see the scale below. No watching plan required, it works just as well as a standalone service, and of course it's also included in all three visit packages.

Fixed and clear, always known in advance. The bigger the job, the lower the percentage we take.

Two examples
Pool filter, €300 invoice → 20% coordination fee = €60
Roof replacement, €8,000 invoice → 10% coordination fee = €800

We never receive money on behalf of the tradesperson, you pay them directly. Our fee is only payment for our own work finding, quoting, approving and following up.

The price above applies to our core zone (the Béziers/Narbonne belt). For houses further away, we give a fixed price at first contact, no hidden distance surcharges later.

Want to rent out the house when you're not there?

We can act as co-host for you, with years of our own experience as an Airbnb Superhost. We don't handle the rental in any other way, bookings and guest payments are handled by you or the platform. Ask us and we'll tell you more about what suits you.

About us

We're a Swedish family who moved to Languedoc and already run Volets France, a joinery workshop that renovates shutters for Scandinavian homeowners in the region. Duco Maison is the same care for craftsmanship and honesty, applied to the whole house, not just the shutters.

David has several years of background as a project manager building exhibition stands, with everything that involves, from drawing and design to ordering, supplier contact and procurement. That's exactly the skill set needed to hold a house together from a distance, not something we picked up along the way.

We know what Scandinavian homeowners expect from finished work, and we know what it feels like to sit at home wondering how the house in France is really doing. That's precisely the worry we exist to remove.

Already a Volets France customer? You already know us, get in touch and we'll set up the home watching at the same time.
