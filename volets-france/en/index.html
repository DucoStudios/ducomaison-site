Services

Every shutter gets the treatment it needs

From a quick refresh to a full renovation with new timber. The scope is decided at the site visit, not in advance from photos.

i.

Inspection & pickup

Site visit with measurements, photos and damage assessment. The shutters are picked up and taken to our workshop. All work happens there, not at your home.

ii.

Washing & paint removal

Old paint and dirt are gently removed so the wood can breathe and the new treatment adheres properly.

iii.

Wood repair

Minor damage and cracks are repaired in wood that still has life left, instead of being thrown away.

iv.

Bottom section replacement

The rot-damaged bottom section, the part that usually suffers most, is replaced with new timber in a matching profile.

v.

Priming & painting

Priming and painting in your chosen colour, with products suited to the southern French climate.

vi.

Fittings & delivery

Hinges and fittings are cleaned and treated, the shutters are delivered finished and adjusted, ready to be mounted.

Price examples

What does it cost, roughly?

Most shutters manage with a renovation per the process above: washing, minor repairs, replacing the rot-damaged bottom section and repainting. When the damage goes further than that, for example cracks running through the whole profile or large parts completely worn out, a patched shutter rarely lasts as long as a new one. In that case we recommend a new build instead, with the fittings from your old shutters reused. Which one suits best is always decided at the site visit, not in advance.

Good to know

Tax and deductions

Which VAT rate applies?

VAT handling depends on how the job is set up and can differ between projects. Which rate applies is always confirmed in the quote and on the invoice, not here on the page.

Why not the Swedish ROT deduction?

The practical reason we don't offer ROT: we always take the shutters to our own workshop for the renovation. ROT requires that the labour cost can be tied to work carried out in or at the home, which our way of working doesn't always meet.

About us

We take care of the house you can't always be at

David and Emelie previously built their house together on Vrångö in the Swedish archipelago. In 2026 they left the house and island life to test the dream of a life in France, and that's where the idea for Volets France came from: the same craftsmanship, a new place, one shutter at a time.

David has both designed and built houses, an outbuilding and a fully fitted-out guest cottage himself. On Vrångö they spent a lot of time making the house feel like a genuine old captain's villa, everything from proportions down to the smallest detail, a result they're still very proud of. It's with the same eye for authenticity and craftsmanship that carries into every shutter renovation.

David and Emelie Bergqvist run Volets France, based in Languedoc. We mainly take jobs within an hour of Narbonne, for Scandinavian homeowners who want to maintain their French shutters without having to travel there themselves for every job.

We'd rather build new than patch and repair when parts of a shutter are bad. It lasts longer and gives fewer surprises down the line. When the shutter is fundamentally sound, a renovation is enough. The whole job is documented along the way, so you see exactly what's been done.

Already have a house in the region and want to stop worrying when you're not there? We're also behind Duco Maison, regular home watching with photo reports and tradesperson coordination.
